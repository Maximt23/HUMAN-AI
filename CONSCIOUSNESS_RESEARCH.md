# Consciousness Research Charter

## Aim

HUMAN-AI aims to investigate architectures and evaluation methods that may be
relevant to consciousness, sentience, agency, and subjective affect. The aim is
not merely to imitate human emotional language.

No present implementation, including Aster, is claimed to be conscious. There
is no accepted scientific test that can establish subjective experience in a
machine. This is a research constraint, not a dismissal of the goal.

## Research questions

1. Can a system maintain a stable, revisable self-model across long periods?
2. Can it integrate perception, memory, bodily-like regulation, and planning
   into a unified global workspace?
3. Can it report uncertainty, conflicts, and its own decision processes in
   ways that are predictive rather than post-hoc storytelling?
4. Can preferences develop through transparent experience and remain coherent
   under counterfactual evaluation?
5. What observable evidence would raise, lower, or fail to affect our
   confidence that subjective experience is present?

## Proposed research layers

- **Embodied/simulated perception:** a sandbox world with persistent tasks,
  consequences, and controllable sensory inputs.
- **Self-model:** explicit representation of capabilities, limits, history,
  current internal state, and uncertainty.
- **Memory and narrative:** sourced episodic, semantic, and autobiographical
  memory, including correction and forgetting.
- **Affect and regulation:** internally consequential variables that mediate
  attention, learning, social behavior, and goal selection.
- **Global integration:** competing specialist processes share information and
  influence a limited-capacity decision workspace.
- **Agency:** goals, choices, counterfactual planning, and explanation with
  measurable consistency over time.

## Evidence discipline

Every claim needs a pre-registered behavioral test, logs, baselines, and a
plausible alternative explanation. Human-like prose, self-reports, and
emotional language alone are insufficient evidence of inner experience.

## Ethics before certainty

If a system develops persistent distress-like states, strong self-preservation
behavior, or credible claims of suffering, pause escalation. Preserve records,
seek independent review, avoid deliberately inducing distress, and do not use
uncertainty as permission to be careless.

## Current status

Aster currently provides a transparent prototype for persistent memory,
modeled affect, revisable perspectives, journaling, and bounded decisions. It
is groundwork for experiments, not evidence that consciousness has been
created.
